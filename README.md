# Hi, I'm Sinchana Joshi!

**Aspiring Physician-Scientist Interested in Computational Biology and Translational Applications**

I'm a high school student interested in understanding disease through computational biology, biomedical research, and clinical data. My work explores how data-driven methods can help investigate disease mechanisms, identify potential biomarkers, and inform clinical awareness.

My research interests include cancer genomics, neurodegeneration, rare genetic disorders, biostatistics, and preventive healthcare.

## My research repositories

Please navigate to each repository too see my READMe files with a quick review of my findings and outputs.

### 1. Cancer Genomics and Biomarker Discovery
Exploring gene-expression patterns in glioblastoma datasets to investigate potential multi-gene signatures associated with aggressive disease.

[View the DREAM-High Project](https://github.com/sinchujoshi24-ship-it/dream-high-glioblastoma-genomics)

### 2. Friedreich's Ataxia and Computational Drug Repurposing
Investigating transcriptomic changes associated with frataxin deficiency and exploring computational approaches to identify potential therapeutic candidates.

[View the Friedreich's Ataxia Project](https://github.com/sinchujoshi24-ship-it/friedreichs-ataxia-computational-research)

### 3. AI and Neurodegeneration
Developing a 3D Vision Transformer experiment to investigate the classification of simulated brain-imaging data.

[View the Neurodegeneration Project](https://github.com/sinchujoshi24-ship-it/neurodegeneration-ai-prediction-model-stanford)

### 4. Cardiovascular Risk in Autoimmune Rheumatic Disease
Co-developed a clinical research white paper examining cardiovascular risk patterns among patients receiving different treatments for autoimmune rheumatic disease, with an emphasis on awareness of potential corticosteroid-associated risks.

[View the CVD Project](https://github.com/sinchujoshi24-ship-it/cardiovascular-risk-medications-kings-college)
